## Draft day shapes

### 1. Old City Icons
- **Mode:** walk
- **Duration:** half day (3–4 hours)
- **Description:** Self-guided loop of the must-see Lanna temples inside the moat, starting at Tha Phae Gate.
- **Locations (ordered):** Tha Phae Gate → Wat Chiang Man → Three Kings Monument → Wat Chedi Luang → Wat Phan Tao → Wat Phra Singh
- **Why:** Classic walking-tour / first-day package shape for the walled city.

### 2. Markets & Wat Ket
- **Mode:** walk
- **Duration:** half day (2–3 hours)
- **Description:** Local commerce and riverside temple life just east of the Old City — the other half of “in-town” Chiang Mai.
- **Locations (ordered):** Warorot Market → Ton Lam Yai Market → Wat Ket Karam → Nawarat Bridge riverside
- **Why:** Markets appear constantly in city itineraries; kept separate so Old City stays temple-focused.

### 3. Doi Suthep & Forest Temple
- **Mode:** scooter
- **Duration:** half day (3–5 hours)
- **Description:** Chiang Mai’s sacred mountain temple plus the forest “hidden temple” on the same ridge road.
- **Locations (ordered):** Wat Pha Lat → Wat Phra That Doi Suthep
- **Why:** #1 package morning combo; independently scooterable on the paved mountain road.

### 4. Sticky Waterfalls North
- **Mode:** scooter
- **Duration:** half–full day (4–6 hours round trip)
- **Description:** Ride north to climb the limestone “sticky” falls and the adjacent sacred springs park.
- **Locations (ordered):** Bua Tong Sticky Waterfall → Nam Phu Chet Si
- **Why:** Extremely common full-day tour leg; split from Doi Suthep for self-guided geography/time (west mountain vs ~60 km north).

### 5. Mae Sa Loop Sampler
- **Mode:** scooter
- **Duration:** full day
- **Description:** The classic Mae Rim / Samoeng scenic day: waterfall, botanic garden, and mountain viewpoint — without elephant camps.
- **Locations (ordered):** Mae Sa Waterfall → Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden → Mon Jam
- **Why:** Reddit and scooter guides push Samoeng/Mae Sa as the first “real” ride day; stripped of tour-model animal stops.

### 6. Doi Inthanon (trail group — 3 variants)
- **Mode:** scooter
- **Duration:** full day
- **Status in DB:** trail group `Doi Inthanon` with variants Classic / Kew Mae Pan / Pha Dok Siew (unpublished)
- **Classic (required):** Summit → Ang Ka → Royal Pagodas → Wachirathan  
  **Optional:** Sirithan, Mae Klang Waterfall, Baan Khun Klang (lunch)
- **Kew Mae Pan (required):** Summit → Ang Ka → Kew Mae Pan → Wachirathan  
  **Optional:** Royal Pagodas (seasonal ridge walk; guide required)
- **Pha Dok Siew (required):** Royal Pagodas → Pha Dok Siew → Baan Mae Klang Luang → Wachirathan  
  **Optional:** Mae Klang Waterfall
- **Atlas only (not on these variants):** Mae Ya Waterfall (long detour)
- **Why:** Tour packages offer 1–3 day shapes; mutually exclusive long hikes are variants; skippable extras are optional stops.

## Access notes

- Walk vs scooter preferences: dense moat/markets = walk; anything west up Doi Suthep, north to Sticky Falls, Mae Rim loop, or Doi Inthanon = scooter
- Tour-only / operator-dependent rejects: elephant sanctuaries, packaged ATV/rafting bundles
- Parking / road / permit notes: Doi Suthep paved but switchbacks (confident riders); Sticky Falls mostly highway then short lane; Doi Inthanon long climb — prefer 150cc+ and early start; national park fees at Inthanon / some waterfall sites
- Scooter not required for trails 1–2

## Package / source notes

- Klook / local operators: Doi Suthep + Wat Pha Lat + Sticky Waterfall (+ orchid farm filler) full-day vans
- Walking guides: Old City temple loops (Phra Singh, Chedi Luang, Chiang Man, Phan Tao, Tha Phae)
- Reddit r/chiangmai + scooter guides: Samoeng/Mae Sa loop as first ride day; Doi Suthep half-day; Sticky Waterfalls as its own outing; Doi Inthanon optional long day; don’t stack Suthep + Samoeng + reservoir in one day
- Refined by splitting the mega “Suthep + Sticky” package into two self-guided days
